Intraword underscores stay literal: snake_case_name and __dunder__init__.

Intraword asterisks do emphasise: aster*isk*s.

Triple markers: ***bold italic***, and ___also bold italic___.

Mixed nesting: **bold with *italic* inside**, and *italic with **bold** inside*.

Overlapping (not nestable): *a **b* c**.

Adjacent runs: **a****b** and *a**b*.

Emphasis touching punctuation: **"quoted"**, *(parenthesised)*, **end.**

Emphasis around a link: **[bold link](https://example.com)** and
[*italic link text*](https://example.com).

Strikethrough combined: ~~**struck bold**~~ and **~~bold struck~~**.

An unmatched marker: **not closed

A single asterisk on its own: *

Emphasis with an underscore inside a word boundary: _leading_ and trailing_.

## Variant A — link in the post

Use this one. It is simpler, and the attribution is clean.

---

Your LLM answers in Markdown. The person who asked for it wants a Word document.

That gap costs everyone I work with a few minutes a day. Paste the raw answer
into Word and you get literal `## Heading` down the page. Paste the rendered
answer and it looks fine until someone applies the company template and nothing
changes — because the formatting is painted onto the text rather than coming
from a style.

So we built the missing step, and put it on the site for free:

https://ksprtech.com/tools/markdown-to-word?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=mdword-launch

Paste Markdown, get a real .docx. Every heading is a genuine Word heading style,
so templates apply, the navigation pane works, and References → Table of
Contents builds itself. Tables, footnotes and equations survive — equations
arrive as native Word equations you can click and edit, not pictures.

The conversion runs inside your browser tab. Your document is never uploaded,
which is the part that mattered to us, because most of what we convert is client
work.

No signup, no limits, no upsell. It joins the other free tools on ksprtech.com
and, like the rest, it exists because we needed it.

## Notes

- **Character budget:** LinkedIn truncates at roughly 200 characters behind a
  "see more". Variant A's first two lines carry the whole hook, so the post
  works even for people who never expand it. Do not reorder them.
- **Do not say "no network requests" without qualifying it.** The version above
  says the conversion runs in the tab and the document is never uploaded, which
  is true. The page itself loads analytics; see the invariants in
  [`README.md`](README.md).
- **No metrics.** No user counts, no "trusted by", no star count. There are
  none, and this is the audience most likely to know that.
- **Reply to every comment for the first day.** LinkedIn weights early
  engagement heavily, and this is the channel where your actual network is.
- If the company page and your personal profile both post, **stagger them by a
  day** and do not use identical text. Reposting the company post from the
  personal profile is fine and is not the same thing.
